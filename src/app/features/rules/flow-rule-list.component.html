Money in and transfers

What a transaction counts as in the reports when its description contains a keyword

Without a matching rule, money out is spending, money in is income on a chequing account and a refund on a credit card, and everything on a savings account is a transfer.

When several keywords match, the longest wins. Income and refund rules only apply to money in; transfer and ignored rules apply both ways.
